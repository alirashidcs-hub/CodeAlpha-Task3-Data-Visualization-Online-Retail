# Visualizations Index

All charts below are generated by `src/visualization.py` (or the notebook)
directly from the prepared Online Retail dataset. Regenerate at any time with:

```bash
python src/visualization.py
```

## Required Visualizations (1–10)

| File | Chart | Analytical Question Answered |
|---|---|---|
| `01_revenue_by_country.png` | Total revenue by country (top 10) | Which countries generate the most revenue? |
| `02_top_products.png` | Top 10 products by revenue vs. by quantity sold | Which products drive revenue vs. volume — are they the same? |
| `03_monthly_revenue.png` | Monthly revenue trend, Dec 2010–Dec 2011 | How does revenue change over time? |
| `04_daily_revenue.png` | Total revenue by day of week | Which days generate the most revenue? |
| `05_hourly_revenue.png` | Total revenue by hour of day | When during the day does trading activity peak? |
| `06_transaction_distribution.png` | Distribution of invoice revenue | What does a "typical" order look like, and how skewed is order value? |
| `07_quantity_price_relationship.png` | Quantity vs. unit price scatter (sampled) | Does price influence how much customers buy? |
| `08_cancellation_analysis.png` | Cancellation rate by month and by country | How common are cancellations, and do they vary by market? |
| `09_customer_analysis.png` | Orders, revenue, and average order value per customer | How does customer value vary across the customer base? |
| `10_rfm_analysis.png` | Recency, Frequency, Monetary distributions | What does customer purchase behavior look like through an RFM lens? |
